Quick answers

Things people ask before their first order

Still wondering? Ask the helper in the corner or read the full FAQ.

How much is shipping?

A flat $10.95 anywhere in Australia, and free when your order is $75 or more. Prices include GST.

Will it really carry 14 kg?

Yes. The shopping and shoulder bags are rated to 14 kg and guaranteed for at least 5 years of daily or weekly use. If the stitching fails in that time, we replace it.

How do I wash it?

Quick machine wash, line dry, cool iron only if you must. It dries in minutes.

What happens when it finally wears out?

Post it back to us. The nylon is endlessly recyclable, so it never needs to see landfill.
