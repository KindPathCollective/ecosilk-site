The fine print

Shipping, returns & privacy

Written to be read. If anything's unclear, ask us.

Shipping

We ship Australia-wide via Australia Post for a flat $10.95 per order, and free on orders of $75 or more. Orders are packed in the Northern Rivers, NSW within 1–2 business days. Delivery then typically takes 2–6 business days depending on your location. You'll receive a confirmation email when your order is placed. International orders are shipped by quote; contact us with your address.

Returns & guarantee

Changed your mind? Return unused, unwashed bags within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the bag price (return postage is at your cost unless we sent the wrong item). Email us first so we know to expect it.

Every Ecosilk shopping and shoulder bag is guaranteed for a minimum of 5 years with daily or weekly use. If the stitching or fabric fails in that time, email a photo and we'll replace the bag. Normal fading from sun exposure or damage from sharp objects isn't covered, but we'll still happily recycle the bag for you.

Recycling

Worn-out bags can be posted to Ecosilk Bags, 159 McClelland Rd, Barkers Vale NSW 2474. The nylon is recycled rather than landfilled.

Privacy

We collect the details needed to fulfil your order (name, address, email, phone) and, if you opt in, your email for occasional updates. Payments are processed by PayPal; we never see or store your card details. Order details are sent to us by email through Formspree and kept for fulfilment, warranty and tax purposes. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing info@ecosilkbags.com.au.

This site uses only functional storage in your browser (your bag contents and theme preference). If analytics are enabled, they are configured without advertising features.

Terms

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. We reserve the right to correct pricing errors before dispatch; if that happens we'll contact you before charging anything. Discount codes can't be combined with each other unless stated, and WELCOME15 is for first orders. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
