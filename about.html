Our impact

A bag designed in 1999 to make plastic bags extinct

Emily Hay wasn't trying to start a brand. She was trying to stop reaching for plastic. Twenty-five years on, the design has barely changed because it hasn't needed to.

The material

Why parachute silk

Emily chose nylon parachute fabric for the things that matter in a bag you'll use for a decade: it's extraordinarily strong for its weight, quick-drying, colourfast, and resistant to water, mould and abrasion. It scrunches down to almost nothing. And unlike most "eco" fabrics, it's recyclable not once or twice, but endlessly.

It is not actual silk, and no animals are involved. The name comes from the way the fabric feels.

Honest about where it's made

Australian owned and designed. Made in China, properly.

Ecosilk is 100% Australian owned and designed from the Northern Rivers of New South Wales. The bags are sewn in China by a long-standing partner, to a high standard and under ethical working conditions. We'd rather say that plainly than hide it.

The stitching methods were chosen for strength over speed, which is why we can guarantee the bags for five years and why so many are still in use after fifteen.

The story so far

Small company, long memory

  1. 1999

    Emily Hay settles on parachute-silk nylon after testing fabrics for strength, weight and recyclability.

  2. 2001

    The original Ecosilk shopping bag goes on sale, built specifically to replace single-use plastic bags.

  3. Shoulder bags, drawstrings, produce bags

    The range grows to five styles in up to 24 colours, plus Reef Collection colour packs named after the species they help protect.

  4. Today

    Stocked by health food stores, post offices and gift shops across Australia and overseas, and a business supporter of the Australian Marine Conservation Society.
