Ordering & shipping

How much is shipping and how long does it take?

Shipping is a flat $10.95 anywhere in Australia, and free when your order is $75 or more. We pack from the Northern Rivers, NSW within 1–2 business days; Australia Post then takes roughly 2–6 business days depending on where you are.

Do you ship overseas?

Yes, by quote. Send us your address and what you'd like via the contact page and we'll come back with a price.

Do prices include GST?

Yes. Every price on the site is GST-inclusive, and your confirmation shows the GST component.

How do I pay?

Through PayPal, which also accepts Visa, Mastercard and Pay in 4 without a PayPal account. Your card details never touch our site.

Can I return a bag?

Unused bags can be returned within 30 days for a refund of the bag price. Faulty bags are covered by the 5-year guarantee at any time — just email us a photo.

The bags

Will it really carry 14 kg?

Yes. The shopping and shoulder bags are rated to 14 kg. Your arm gives out before the bag does.

What are the sizes?

Shopping bag: 55 cm long × 32.5 cm wide, expanding to 52 cm with the gusset. Shoulder / beach bag: 70 cm × 32.5 cm, same gusset. Drawstring bag: 27 cm × 16 cm. The mini drawstring holds one folded shopping bag.

Is it actually silk?

No. 'Parachute silk' is the common name for the lightweight nylon used in parachutes. It feels silky, weighs next to nothing, and is endlessly recyclable.

What's the guarantee?

Every shopping and shoulder bag is guaranteed for a minimum of 5 years with daily or weekly use. If the stitching fails in that time, we replace the bag.

How do I wash it?

Quick machine wash, line dry, cool iron only if you must. It dries in minutes and the colour holds.

Where are they made?

Ecosilk is 100% Australian owned and designed by Emily Hay. The bags are made in China to a high standard under ethical working conditions.

Saving & gifting

How do the discounts work?

Add any 3 or more single bags (in any colours) and 10% comes off those bags automatically. Orders over $75 ship free. New customers can use WELCOME15 for 15% off a first order. Four or more single shoulder bags also earn a free drawstring bag.

Which bag makes the best gift?

The Reef Collection packs: four or six coordinated colours named after a reef species, ready to hand over. A mini drawstring bag also works as reusable gift wrap for a single shopping bag. Tick 'this is a gift' at checkout and we leave prices off the packing slip.

Can you print our logo on bags?

Yes. We print artwork and logos on shopping, shoulder and fruit & vegie bags for fundraising, events and promotions. See the Fundraising & custom page.

End of life

What do I do with a worn-out bag?

Post it back to Ecosilk Bags, 159 McClelland Rd, Barkers Vale NSW 2474. The nylon is recycled, so your bag never goes to landfill.
